# Where the product stands, 2026-08-28

Everything below was measured on this date. Nothing is projected.

## The two numbers

| | hold-out (59 parts) | blind corpus (43 parts) |
|---|---|---|
| READ | 56/59 (95%) | 36/43 (84%) |
| SHIPS | 55/59 (93%) | 30/43 (70%) |
| flagged per shipping part | **1.40** | **1.73** |
| worst part | 4 | 4 |
| nothing to check | 17/45 | 6/30 |
| gate: never above 5 | MET | MET |

The blind corpus is new: 43 datasheets fetched from vendor sites on this date for
parts in neither existing corpus, weighted towards vendors the others are
thinnest on (Frontgrade ceramic rad-hard, Diodes, Nexperia, Renesas, Microsemi,
plus TI packages nothing here had seen). It is harder than the hold-out and the
gap is the honest measure of how much the tuned corpus flatters us.

Both figures improved by 0.4 during the day for a reason that was not a product
change: `bench:blind` and `bench:holdout` called `shipOutcome` on a record the
product would have enriched with `withPrintedFootprint` first. The instruments
were measuring something the product does not do.

## What the copper was checked against

Three independent sources, none of which share a failure mode.

**The datasheet's own two pages.** `bench:joints` lays the lead from the package
outline drawing onto the land from the printed footprint and asks whether there
is metal underneath. Nothing in this project had ever done that: `leadWidthMm`
was read on 39 parts and never once compared to the copper it lands on.

- 89 footprints overlaid, 3 with a lead off its copper.
- **0 of 30 shipping parts in the blind corpus.** That is the check's false
  positive rate on documents nobody has looked at.
- It found ADXL345 emitting 0.25 mm lands under 0.50 mm terminals, on a part
  shipping four files.

**KiCad's official library.** `bench:published` matches our footprints to theirs
on pin count, pitch, body and lead form, and compares pad for pad.

```
span    median  +0.000   range -1.000 to +0.550
length  median  -0.100   range -0.500 to +0.704
width   median   0.000   range -0.350 to +0.070
```

One footprint outside what house style explains, and the worst width
disagreement in the whole set is ADXL345 at -0.350: a third source landing on
the same defect.

**The hand-read oracles.** 0 false confirmations on the pinout, 0 on the copper.
No value ships silently that a hand-read drawing disagrees with.

## Live defects found and fixed

1. **ADXL345 shipped a land half the width of its terminal.** `bench:dimensions`
   had the reading marked WRONG the whole time; `bench:copper` compares pads to
   the record and the record was consistently wrong. Now flagged to the user with
   both numbers.
2. **The verification panel reported every dimension as "not read"** for any
   family datasheet, under a green "Ready to build" banner, while the exported
   files carried all of them. It rendered the flat block; the product builds from
   the per-package table.
3. **The package chooser offered 2 of 5 packages** on OPA333 and stated "2 were
   read from this datasheet" when five were.
4. **Two forming-die settings blocked every first run**, for every part,
   including plastic packages they do not apply to, behind a primary button that
   silently refused. An engineer testing the product invented two numbers to get
   past it.
5. **Bus-range pin names shipped as net names.** UT7R995 emitted eight outputs
   sharing the name `nQ[1:0]`. The name is now dropped and the number kept.
6. **The reader's refusals were reported as facts about the document** ("the
   document does not state them", beside the pages that state them), in raw
   schema paths, prefixed with the vendor's model id on every line.

## What a stranger said after using it

A subagent with no context drove the app end to end and got a correct library
out. Its verdict on the footprint: pads, pitch, courtyard, mask margin and every
pin name matched TI's drawings exactly, and the eight checks in `manifest.json`
are "the right eight checks, phrased the way an engineer thinks".

Its verdict on the screen, before the fixes above: *"I cannot put a footprint on
a flight board on the word of a tool that contradicts itself that badly in the
one panel meant to let me verify it."*

Both halves of that are worth keeping. The engineering was sound and the surface
that proves it was not.

## Known open, not fixed

- **`tps7a8300`** emits a 3.90 mm centre span where the same VQFN-20 package with
  the same body, lead span and land size emits 4.65 elsewhere. Flagged to the
  user twice with the page cited, so nothing ships silently. Not chased: it is a
  blind-corpus document and the rule is that those are not opened to diagnose.
- **Two blind-corpus parts** carry a lead-on-land finding that has not been
  adjudicated, for the same reason.
- **Every pin ships as `electricalType: unspecified`**, so KiCad ERC has nothing
  to work with.
- **No footprint preview** anywhere in the app.
- **6 of 36 read parts do not ship**, all of them short a land pattern number the
  document did not print.

## What no amount of software reaches

Two things, and neither is an information problem:

- whether an engineer finds 1.40 to 1.73 glances per part acceptable
- whether the paste and the oven behave

Both need a person with a board.
